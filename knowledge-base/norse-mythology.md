# Norse Mythology — Knowledge Base

## Gods and Deities
- Odin: Allfather, god of wisdom, war, and death. Sacrificed his eye for wisdom
- Thor: God of thunder, lightning, and strength. Wields the hammer Mjolnir
- Loki: Trickster god, shape-shifter, agent of chaos
- Freya: Goddess of love, fertility, war, and magic
- Tyr: God of law and heroic glory, sacrificed his hand
- Baldur: God of light, purity, and beauty, killed by mistletoe
- Heimdall: Watchman of the gods, guardian of Bifrost
- Frigg: Goddess of foresight and wisdom, wife of Odin

## Sacred Places
- Asgard: Realm of the gods, connected to other worlds by Bifrost
- Midgard: The human world, protected by Thor
- Jotunheim: Land of the giants, enemies of the gods
- Niflheim: World of ice, mist, and cold
- Valhalla: Hall of the slain, where brave warriors feast with Odin
- Yggdrasil: The world tree connecting all nine realms

## Magic and Powers
- Runes: Magical symbols with power over fate and nature
- Seidr: A form of magic practiced by Odin and Freya
- Berserker rage: Warriors channeling bear or wolf spirits in battle
- Mjolnir: Thor hammer, returns when thrown, only worthy can lift it
- Gungnir: Odin spear, never misses its target

## Culture and Society
- Vikings were fierce warriors and skilled seafarers
- Honor and glory in battle was the highest virtue
- Ragnarok: The prophesied end of the world and rebirth
- Norns: Three sisters who weave the fate of gods and men
- Einherjar: Warriors chosen by Valkyries to fight at Ragnarok

## Architecture
- Longhouses: Large communal halls for feasting and shelter
- Stave churches: Wooden churches with intricate carvings
- Runestones: Large stones carved with runic inscriptions
- Great halls: Mead halls where warriors gathered to feast
