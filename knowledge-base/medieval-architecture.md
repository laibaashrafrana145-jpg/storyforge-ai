# Medieval Architecture and World-Building — Knowledge Base

## Castle Architecture
- Keep: The main tower and last line of defense
- Curtain walls: Outer defensive walls surrounding the castle
- Drawbridge: Movable bridge over the moat
- Portcullis: Heavy iron gate that drops to block the entrance
- Battlements: Defensive parapets on top of walls with gaps for archers
- Moat: Water-filled ditch surrounding the castle for defense

## City Layout
- Market square: Central gathering place for trade and announcements
- Guild halls: Buildings where craftsmen organized their trades
- Cathedral: The dominant religious building at the city center
- City walls: Defensive stone walls surrounding medieval cities
- Cobblestone streets: Stone-paved roads through the city
- Taverns: Gathering places for travelers and locals

## Social Structure
- King: Absolute ruler, considered divinely appointed
- Lords and nobles: Landowners who owed military service to the king
- Knights: Armored warriors who served the nobility
- Peasants and serfs: Farmers who worked the land
- Clergy: Priests and monks who served the church
- Merchants: Traders who grew wealthy through commerce

## Magic Systems in Fantasy Worlds
- Ley lines: Invisible lines of magical energy crossing the landscape
- Magic towers: Isolated structures where mages study and practice
- Enchanted forests: Ancient woods with protective or dangerous magic
- Cursed artifacts: Objects imbued with dark magic from past events
- Magical guilds: Organizations that train and regulate magic users
- Elemental magic: Powers drawn from fire, water, earth, and air
